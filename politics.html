Politics & Advocacy

Participation should lead to influence.

Tommy’s political work connects Northern Ireland with a wider European liberal movement, with a focus on advocacy, organisational capacity and meaningful youth participation.

Political and civic work.

2026–28

LYMEC Bureau

Serving as Advocacy and Outreach Officer for European Liberal Youth. Reponsible for overseeing the organisation's overarching advocacy strategy, relationships with external partners, and the Individual Members' Section. Regional responsibilities include the UK and Ireland, Benelux and France.

2023–26

Alliance Youth

As International Officer, Tommy developed relationships with liberal youth organisations, contributed to policy and represented Alliance Youth at international events. He was also an Alliance Party candidate in the 2023 local elections.

2024–25

Young European Movement UK

As Head of Finance and Administration, Tommy supported the day-to-day running of the organisation, advised on budgets and represented YEM UK externally.

2019–23

UK-German Connection

Following the “Peacing it Together II” seminar, Tommy served for three years on the Alumni Youth Council and helped shape programmes supporting exchange, dialogue and cooperation.
